II. Use Case Specifications

UC-01 Sign In / Sign Out

Primary Actors
Registered User (Patient, Dentist, Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner, Administrator)
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Authenticate an existing account, establish a session with the permissions of the account, and end the session on sign-out.
Preconditions
Sign-in: an active account exists. Sign-out: an authenticated session exists.
Normal Flow
A. Sign In
1. The user opens the Sign-in screen of the customer area or of the staff area and enters an e-mail or user name and a password.
2. DCMS validates the credentials and checks the status of the account.
3. DCMS establishes a session and shows the functions permitted for the account (the customer home, or the staff menu built from the permissions). The sign-in flow ends.
B. Sign Out
1. The authenticated user selects Sign Out (this device or all devices).
2. DCMS invalidates the session.
3. DCMS returns the user to the unauthenticated interface. The sign-out flow ends.
Alternative Flows
A1 — Account recovery, at Sign In step 1: The user selects "Quên mật khẩu?" and continues with UC-04. UC-01 ends without a session.
A2 — Sign in with Google, at Sign In step 1: The user selects the Google button and continues with UC-02.
A3 — Idle time, during a staff session: When the user has not acted for the idle time set in the general settings, DCMS ends the session and returns the user to Sign-in.
Exception Flows
E1 — Invalid credentials or unavailable account, at Sign In step 2: DCMS displays MSG07 without saying which credential or which account condition caused the failure. No session is created.
E2 — The service is unavailable, at Sign In step 2 or 3: DCMS displays MSG49 and does not report a successful sign-in.
E3 — The session has expired when Sign Out is requested: DCMS displays MSG06 and returns the user to the unauthenticated interface.
E4 — A required field is empty, at Sign In step 1: DCMS shows "Vui lòng nhập email hoặc tên đăng nhập." or "Vui lòng nhập mật khẩu." and does not send the request.
Postconditions
1. Sign-in succeeded: a session exists.
2. Sign-out succeeded: the session is invalid and cannot authorize further requests.
3. Sign-in failed: no new session exists.
Business Rule
GBR-001

 

UC-02 Sign In with Google

Primary Actors
Patient (Guest)
Secondary ActorsGoogle Identity Service
Description
Authenticate a customer with a Google account. A customer who signs in with Google for the first time gets a new customer account.
Preconditions
Google sign-in is configured on the server (client ID). The customer has a Google account.
Normal Flow
1. The customer opens Customer Sign-in and selects the Google button.
2. Google asks the customer to choose an account and returns an identity token to DCMS.
3. DCMS verifies the token with Google.
4. If the Google identity is already linked to a customer account, DCMS uses that account.
5. If it is not linked, DCMS checks that the e-mail of the Google account does not belong to another account, creates a customer account (role Guest, e-mail confirmed) and links the Google identity to it.
6. DCMS establishes a session and opens the customer home.
Alternative Flows
A1 — Google sign-in is not configured, at step 1: The screen shows "Đăng nhập Google chưa được cấu hình trên máy chủ." and the user uses UC-01.
Exception Flows
E1 — The token is invalid, or the linked account is not a customer account, at step 3 or 4: DCMS displays MSG07 and creates no session.
E2 — The e-mail already belongs to another account, at step 5: DCMS refuses and asks the customer to sign in with the method used before, or to choose another Google account. No account is created.
E3 — Google cannot be loaded, or returns no credential, at step 2: The screen shows "Không tải được Google. Vui lòng kiểm tra kết nối." or "Google không trả về thông tin đăng nhập.".
E4 — The service is unavailable, at step 3 to 6: DCMS displays MSG49.
Postconditions
1. Success: a session exists; a new customer account exists only in the case of step 5, with an audit record.
2. Failure: no session and no new account.
Business Rule
GBR-003

 

UC-03 Register Patient Account

Primary Actors
Guest
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Create a customer account with a Vietnamese mobile number, an e-mail and a password that follows the password policy of the system.
Preconditions
The visitor has no account with the same phone number or e-mail. The password policy is configured.
Normal Flow
1. The visitor opens Customer Register from the Access Portal or from Customer Sign-in.
2. The visitor enters the full name, the phone number, the e-mail, the password and the password confirmation. DCMS shows the password policy.
3. DCMS validates each field and the password against the policy.
4. The visitor selects "Đăng ký".
5. DCMS creates the customer account (role Guest) and displays MSG08.
6. DCMS opens Customer Sign-in.
Alternative Flows
A1 — The visitor already has an account, at step 1: The visitor selects "Đăng nhập" and continues with UC-01.
Exception Flows
E1 — A required field is empty or the format is wrong (phone number not a mobile number of 10 digits, e-mail invalid), at step 3: DCMS displays MSG02 or MSG04 under the field.
E2 — The input is too long (name over 160 characters, e-mail over 254), at step 3: DCMS displays MSG03.
E3 — The password does not follow the policy, or the confirmation differs, at step 3: DCMS displays MSG39 or "Xác nhận mật khẩu không khớp.".
E4 — The phone number or e-mail already belongs to an account, at step 5: DCMS displays MSG46 with the links to sign in or to recover the account. No account is created.
E5 — The service is unavailable, at step 5: DCMS displays MSG49.
Postconditions
1. Success: a customer account with the role Guest exists and can sign in. It does not create a Patient profile and is not linked to one.
2. Failure: no account is created.
Business Rule
GBR-002

 

UC-04 Recover Account

Primary Actors
Registered User (Patient, Staff)
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Restore access to an existing account through the verification and the credential reset of the system.
Preconditions
The recovery policy is configured. The user knows an identifier of the account. No session is required.
Normal Flow
1. The user opens Recover Account (customer or staff) and enters an account identifier.
2. DCMS displays the same neutral message MSG09 whether or not an eligible account exists.
3. For an eligible account, DCMS sends the recovery instructions through the configured channel.
4. The user follows the instructions and submits the verification proof.
5. DCMS checks that the proof is valid, not expired and not used.
6. The user enters a new password.
7. DCMS checks the password policy and the validity of the proof, replaces the password and displays MSG10.
Alternative Flows
A1 — No eligible account, after step 2: DCMS sends nothing; the public message stays MSG09.
A2 — The user cannot use the verification channel, before step 4: The user asks an Administrator for help, who manages the account outside this use case. This does not reset or unlock the account by itself.
A3 — The user did not receive the proof, at step 4: The user asks DCMS to send it again; DCMS shows MSG09.
Exception Flows
E1 — The proof is invalid, expired or already used, at step 5: DCMS displays MSG38 and does not change the password.
E2 — The new password violates the policy, at step 7: DCMS displays MSG39. The old password stays.
E3 — The instructions cannot be delivered, at step 3: DCMS does not reset the password and still answers with MSG09; the failure is kept for follow-up.
E4 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49.
Postconditions
1. Success: the new password is stored and the recovery is recorded. No session is created.
2. Failure or abandonment: the old password stays.
3. All outcomes: recovery does not unlock a locked account, change roles or link a patient record.
Business Rule
GBR-004

 

UC-05 Manage Staff Accounts

Primary Actors
Admin
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Search, create and edit staff accounts, give them roles, lock and unlock them, and start the recovery of a staff account.
Preconditions
The user is authenticated as Admin (or belongs to an access group with the account permissions).
Normal Flow
1. The Admin opens "Quản lý tài khoản" and sees the accounts with their status (Active or Locked) and roles. The Admin may search by name, user name or code and filter by role and status.
2. The Admin selects "Tạo tài khoản" and enters the user name, full name, employee code, e-mail, phone number, password and roles.
3. The Admin selects "Lưu". DCMS creates the staff account with its employee profile and displays MSG11.
4. To change an account, the Admin opens it, edits the data or the roles and saves; DCMS displays MSG11.
5. To lock or unlock an account, the Admin selects the action and enters the reason. DCMS changes the status, ends all current sessions of a locked account and displays MSG12 or MSG13.
Alternative Flows
A1 — The Admin starts the recovery of a staff account who forgot the password: DCMS sends the verification as in UC-04; the lock status is not changed.
A2 — The Admin opens the history of the account: DCMS lists every change with the actor, the time and the old and new values.
Exception Flows
E1 — A required field is empty, too long or not valid, at step 3: DCMS displays MSG02, MSG03 or MSG04 under the field; a password that does not follow the policy displays MSG39.
E2 — The user name, e-mail, phone number or employee code already belongs to another account, at step 3 or 4: DCMS displays MSG46 under the field.
E3 — The account was changed by someone else, at step 4 or 5: DCMS displays MSG41 and reloads the account.
E4 — The reason of a lock or unlock is missing: DCMS displays MSG02.
E5 — The user has no permission: DCMS displays MSG05.
E6 — The account no longer exists: DCMS displays MSG42.
E7 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49; nothing is changed.
Postconditions
1. Success: the account, its roles and its status are saved and every change is recorded in the account history.
2. A locked account cannot sign in and has no active session.
3. Failure: nothing is changed.
Business Rule
GBR-001, GBR-004, GBR-005, GBR-007

 

UC-06 Manage Access Groups

Primary Actors
Clinic Owner; Admin
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Create and edit access groups (a base role with the screens and actions it may use), assign staff to a group and deactivate a group.
Preconditions
The user is authenticated as Clinic Owner or Admin, or belongs to a group with the permission to manage access groups.
Normal Flow
1. The user opens "Thiết lập hệ thống", section "Phân quyền truy cập", and sees the groups with the code, the name, the base role, the status and the number of staff.
2. The user selects "Tạo nhóm" and enters the code, the name and the base role (Admin, Dentist or Clinic Assistant).
3. The user ticks the screens and the actions of the group; an action can be ticked only with its parent screen.
4. The user selects "Lưu". DCMS saves the group and displays MSG53.
5. To assign staff, the user opens the group, selects the staff and the branches and saves with the reason of the change.
6. To stop using a group, the user deactivates it with a reason.
Alternative Flows
A1 — A user who is not the Clinic Owner opens the grant list: DCMS shows only the grants that the user holds.
A2 — The user edits a group: DCMS keeps the history of the change with the reason.
Exception Flows
E1 — A required field is empty or too long, at step 2: DCMS displays MSG02 or MSG03.
E2 — The code or the name already exists, at step 4: DCMS displays MSG50.
E3 — A grant is not valid, or an action is ticked without its parent screen: DCMS displays MSG68.
E4 — The group still has staff, at step 6, or the change would give the user a right they do not hold or change their own group: DCMS refuses and displays MSG05.
E5 — The group was changed by someone else: DCMS displays MSG41.
E6 — The reason is missing: DCMS displays MSG02.
E7 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49; nothing is changed.
Postconditions
1. Success: the group and its assignments are saved with the reason and the actor; the new rights apply to the staff of the group.
2. Failure: nothing is changed.
Business Rule
GBR-006, GBR-007

 

UC-07 Receive Notifications

Primary Actors
Registered User (Patient, Staff)
Secondary ActorsPush Notification Service
Description
Deliver the notifications of DCMS in real time and as push notifications on the mobile app, and let the user list them, read them and open the related screen.
Preconditions
The user is authenticated. For push notifications, the mobile app has registered a device.
Normal Flow
1. An event occurs (for example an appointment is booked, changed or cancelled, a reminder is due, a support request is answered).
2. DCMS creates a notification for each eligible account and sends it in real time (SignalR) to the open app.
3. For a registered mobile device, DCMS also sends a push notification through the Push Notification Service. The push content does not contain private clinical details.
4. The user opens the notification list and sees the unread count.
5. The user opens a notification. DCMS checks that its link still points to an object the user may open and opens that screen.
6. DCMS marks the notification as read; the user can also mark all notifications as read.
Alternative Flows
A1 — The app is closed, at step 2: The notification stays in the list until the user opens the app; the push notification may reach the device.
A2 — The user turns push notifications off: DCMS keeps the in-app notification and stops the push for that device.
Exception Flows
E1 — The list is empty, at step 4: DCMS displays "Bạn chưa có thông báo nào.".
E2 — The linked object no longer exists or is not accessible, at step 5: DCMS displays MSG42 or MSG05 and does not open it.
E3 — The push cannot be delivered, at step 3: The in-app notification stays valid; the failure is recorded. DCMS displays "Lịch hẹn đã được xác nhận nhưng thông báo chưa gửi được. Nhân viên sẽ kiểm tra." when no channel works.
E4 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49.
Postconditions
1. Notifications are kept in the list with their read status, separate from the delivery attempts.
2. A provider's acceptance of a push does not mean that the user has read it.
Business Rule
GBR-008, GBR-009

 

UC-08 Book Appointment Online

Primary Actors
Patient (Guest)
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Book a confirmed examination for the person who will attend, in five steps: examinee, schedule, contact, review, completion (web and mobile). It also covers: Book an appointment for a saved family member. The booking does not give the booker access to the relative's medical record.
Preconditions
The customer is authenticated. At least one branch accepts bookings, with a published working schedule and an examination service.
Normal Flow
1. The customer opens "Đặt lịch khám" and chooses who will be examined: "Bản thân" (UC-08) or "Người thân" (UC-08).
2. The customer chooses the branch and the dentist ("Bất kỳ bác sĩ (ghế trống)" is allowed) and the date. DCMS shows the free time slots of "Buổi sáng" and "Buổi chiều" for the duration of the examination service and the free chairs.
3. The customer chooses a slot and may add a reason for the visit.
4. The customer confirms the name and the phone number or e-mail of the booker, and the name of the examinee.
5. The customer reviews the branch, the service, the dentist, the time, the booker and the examinee, then selects "Xác nhận đặt lịch".
6. DCMS re-checks, at the time of confirmation, that the selected slot is still available.
7. DCMS creates a Confirmed appointment (for "Bất kỳ bác sĩ", with the dentist of the chosen slot), displays MSG24 and the appointment code.
8. The customer opens the appointment (UC-09) or returns to the home.
Alternative Flows
A1 — No slot matches, at step 2: DCMS displays "Không tìm thấy khung giờ phù hợp. Vui lòng đổi ngày, bác sĩ hoặc dịch vụ."; the customer chooses another date or dentist.
A2 — The same request is sent again after a network error, at step 7: DCMS returns the existing result and displays MSG25; no second appointment is created.
Related function — Book for a Relative:
1. At step 1 of UC-08 the customer chooses "Người thân" and reads the notice that booking does not give access to the relative's record.
2. The customer chooses a family member from "Người thân đã lưu" (search by name). The name and date of birth of the examinee are filled in and cannot be edited.
3. The customer continues with steps 2 to 8 of UC-08. The appointment records the booker and the examinee (relationship "Người thân") separately.
Exception Flows
E1 — Information about the examinee or the booker is missing or wrong, at step 4: DCMS displays MSG18 or MSG02/MSG04 under the field.
E2 — Less than 60 minutes before the start, at step 6: DCMS displays MSG19 and creates nothing.
E3 — The slot is no longer available (taken by another booking, the schedule changed, or no chair is free), at step 6: DCMS displays MSG20, MSG22 or MSG23, loads the slots again and creates nothing.
E4 — The booking data changed meanwhile, at step 6: DCMS displays MSG41 and returns to step 2.
E5 — The session has expired, at step 5 or 6: DCMS displays MSG06 and "Đăng nhập".
E6 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49.
Related function — Book for a Relative (exceptions):
E1 — The saved family member was deleted, at step 2: DCMS shows "Người thân đã xóa hoặc không còn khả dụng. Hãy chọn lại người thân đã lưu." and the customer chooses again.
E2 — The date of birth is invalid or after today: DCMS displays MSG04.
E3 — The customer tries to open the clinical record of the relative from the booking: DCMS displays "Bạn chỉ có thể xem thông tin lịch hẹn do mình đặt; hồ sơ khám của người thân cần được xác minh riêng.".
E4 — Other exceptions follow UC-08.
Postconditions
1. Success: exactly one Confirmed appointment exists with the booker and the examinee recorded separately; its full interval is reserved. No Patient profile, visit or queue entry is created.
2. Failure: no appointment or reservation is created.
3. A failed notification does not cancel the appointment.
Business Rule
GBR-008, GBR-010, GBR-011, GBR-012, GBR-013, GBR-014, GBR-015, GBR-016

 

UC-09 Reschedule or Cancel My Appointment

Primary Actors
Patient (Guest)
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Reschedule or cancel a Confirmed appointment, or send a request to the clinic when the appointment is too close to be changed by the customer, and withdraw a pending request. It also covers: List, filter and open the upcoming and the past appointments that the customer booked, including those booked for relatives (web and mobile).
Preconditions
The customer is authenticated and created the booking (or has the right on it). The appointment is Confirmed and has not started or been checked in.
Normal Flow
1. The customer opens the appointment (UC-09) and selects "Đổi lịch" or "Hủy lịch".
2. DCMS checks the right of the customer, the status of the appointment and that at least 12 hours remain.
3. For a change, the customer chooses a new dentist, date and slot (free slots are loaded) and enters a reason. For a cancellation, the customer enters a reason.
4. DCMS shows a review step. For a cancellation it shows "Bạn có chắc muốn hủy lịch hẹn {} lúc {} không?". The customer confirms.
5. DCMS checks again the appointment and the new slot, releases the old interval only when the new slot is accepted, and records the change in the history.
6. DCMS displays MSG30 (change) or MSG31 (cancellation) and notifies the dentist's calendar and the customer.
Alternative Flows
A1 — Less than 12 hours remain, at step 2: The buttons become "Yêu cầu đổi lịch" or "Yêu cầu hủy lịch". DCMS creates a support request for the staff (UC-20) and displays MSG28. The original appointment stays in effect.
A2 — A request is pending, at step 1: The customer sees "Yêu cầu đang chờ xử lý" and may withdraw it with "Rút yêu cầu". The original appointment stays.
A3 — The customer has no account: The customer contacts the clinic with the booking code (MSG27) and the staff act under UC-17 or UC-18.
Related function — View My Appointments:
1. The customer opens "Lịch hẹn của tôi".
2. The customer chooses "Sắp tới" or "Lịch sử" and may filter by status and by date range.
3. DCMS returns only the appointments booked by the account, with the permitted booking details.
4. The customer opens an appointment. DCMS shows its code, time, branch, service, dentist, booker, examinee, status and the history of changes.
Exception Flows
E1 — No right or the appointment is no longer eligible, at step 2 or 5: DCMS displays MSG05 or MSG26 and changes nothing.
E2 — The new slot is invalid or no longer free, at step 5: DCMS displays MSG29; the original time stays and the customer chooses another slot.
E3 — The appointment changed meanwhile, at step 5: DCMS displays MSG41 and asks the customer to reload.
E4 — The same action was already done: DCMS displays "Thao tác này đã được ghi nhận. Vui lòng kiểm tra kết quả hiện có để tránh ghi trùng.".
E5 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49; nothing is changed.
Related function — View My Appointments (exceptions):
E1 — The date range is wrong ("Đến ngày" before "Từ ngày"), at step 2: DCMS shows "Đến ngày phải từ ngày bắt đầu trở đi." and does not search.
E2 — The account has no right on the appointment, at step 4: DCMS displays MSG05 or MSG37 and shows no private detail.
E3 — The session has expired: DCMS displays MSG06.
E4 — The appointment is no longer available: DCMS displays MSG42 and refreshes the list.
E5 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49 with the button "Thử lại".
Postconditions
1. Change succeeded: the new time is Confirmed, the old interval is released, the history is kept and a linked follow-up stays linked and Booked.
2. Cancellation succeeded: the appointment is Cancelled and the history is kept; an active linked follow-up goes back to "Needs booking".
3. Failure, abandonment or pending request: the original appointment stays in effect.
Business Rule
GBR-008, GBR-017, GBR-018, GBR-019, GBR-020

 

UC-10 Link Account to Patient Record

Primary Actors
Patient (Guest)
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Send a request to the clinic to link the customer account to the patient record of the account owner, follow its status and withdraw it.
Preconditions
The customer is authenticated and has no active self link.
Normal Flow
1. The customer opens "Liên kết hồ sơ".
2. The customer enters the identifying information that the clinic needs (for example the patient code, if known, and information for matching).
3. DCMS validates the request and creates a pending request; it displays MSG15 (or MSG14 when the owner has not yet been checked in).
4. The clinic verifies the request in UC-25.
5. The customer sees the status of the request: waiting for the clinic, verified (MSG16), rejected or revoked (MSG17). When verified, the medical record can be seen (UC-11).
Alternative Flows
A1 — The customer withdraws a pending request, after step 3: DCMS marks it as withdrawn.
A2 — The request is rejected, after step 4: The customer sees the public note and may send a new request.
Exception Flows
E1 — A required field is missing or invalid: DCMS displays MSG02 or MSG04.
E2 — The account or the patient already has an active self link, at step 3: DCMS displays MSG47.
E3 — Ownership cannot be verified: DCMS displays MSG48 (the decision is taken by the staff).
E4 — The session has expired: DCMS displays MSG06.
E5 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49.
Postconditions
1. A request exists with its status; no record is visible before the clinic verifies it.
2. At most one active self link exists for an account.
Business Rule
GBR-002, GBR-022, GBR-023

 

UC-11 View My Medical Record

Primary Actors
Patient
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
See the own medical record that the clinic published: basic information, examinations and results, treatment and payments, images and prescriptions (web and mobile). It also covers: List and open the prescriptions that a dentist has finalised for the customer (web and mobile).
Preconditions
The customer is authenticated and has a Verified self link.
Normal Flow
1. The customer opens "Hồ sơ bệnh án" (web) or the medical record screen (mobile).
2. DCMS checks the account, the self link and the permission of each content.
3. DCMS shows the header with the name and the patient code, and the tabs: "Thông tin cơ bản", "Khám", "Điều trị & Thanh toán", "Hình ảnh", "Đơn thuốc".
4. The customer opens the result of a visit (symptoms, diagnoses, conclusion, advice, return need, disposition, findings, investigation results).
5. The customer opens a published image, or a prescription.
Alternative Flows
A1 — A tab has no published content, at step 3: DCMS displays "Chưa có nội dung bệnh án được phép hiển thị." (for example "Chưa có đơn thuốc").
Related function — View My Prescriptions:
1. The customer opens "Đơn thuốc của tôi".
2. DCMS lists the finalised prescriptions of the linked patient, newest first.
3. The customer opens a prescription and sees the date, the dentist, the diagnosis, each medicine (name, quantity, unit, dose, days, instructions) and the general advice.
Exception Flows
E1 — The account has no Verified self link: DCMS shows "Hãy xác minh liên kết hồ sơ để xem bệnh án." (MSG37) and no data.
E2 — The link was revoked or the account locked, at any step: DCMS stops the reading at once with MSG37.
E3 — An image cannot be opened: DCMS shows "Không mở được hình ảnh." (MSG42).
E4 — The session has expired: DCMS displays MSG06.
E5 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49 with "Thử lại".
Related function — View My Prescriptions (exceptions):
E1 — No Verified self link or the right was revoked: DCMS displays MSG37.
E2 — The prescription is no longer available: DCMS displays MSG42.
E3 — The session has expired: DCMS displays MSG06.
E4 — The service is unavailable: DCMS displays MSG49.
Postconditions
1. The customer has seen only completed and published content of own record.
2. Nothing is created or changed.
Business Rule
GBR-024

UC-13 Manage Family Members

Primary Actors
Patient (Guest)
Secondary ActorsNone
Description
Add, edit, search and delete the family members saved in the account, to use them when booking (web and mobile).
Preconditions
The customer is authenticated.
Normal Flow

Exception Flows

(HTTP 400); an amount outside the limit is refused with "Số tiền không hợp lệ." or "Số tiền vượt giới hạn.". A deposit amount that is not valid is refused with "Số tiền cọc không hợp lệ.".
E2 — A line or its due amount changed meanwhile: DCMS refuses with "Dịch vụ hoặc số còn phải thu đã thay đổi. Vui lòng tải lại." (HTTP 409).
E3 — The 30% lab priority is not met: DCMS refuses with "Cần ưu tiên đủ 30% phí labo trước khi thu cho dịch vụ khác." and records nothing.
E4 — The request key was already used for a different collection: DCMS refuses with "Mã yêu cầu đã được dùng cho một khoản thu khác."; the same request repeated returns the existing result without recording it twice.
E5 — The PayOS webhook has an invalid signature or data: DCMS rejects it (HTTP 400) and records nothing.
E6 — The user is not the owner: the request is refused (HTTP 403).
E7 — The server fails: the transaction is rolled back and nothing is recorded.
Postconditions
1. Success: each collection is written once in the finance ledger inside a serializable transaction that locks the treatment sheet, so two collections cannot both use the same due amount.
2. A repeated request with the same key does not create a second collection.
3. Failure: nothing is recorded as paid.
Business Rule
GBR-056, GBR-058, GBR-062, GBR-063, GBR-064, GBR-065, GBR-066, GBR-067
