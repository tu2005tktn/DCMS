## Revision 16 October 2026 — recurring employee template

The updated v2 UC-03/S09 and GBR-001 supersede the one-week editor described below. S09 edits an employee-wide Monday–Sunday template, with one eligible branch or Not working per weekday, multiple nonoverlapping spans/breaks, and half-hour time entry. Slot starts use the configurable branch interval anchored at each Work start; durations are integer minutes 1–480. See [clinic booking settings](CLINIC-SLOTS-AND-SERVICE-DURATIONS.vi.md). Templates take effect from a selected clinic-local date and repeat until the next effective template. A dated exception replaces that entire date across all branches, including an empty absence. Exceptions do not repeat. Published revisions are retained; changing a historical effective date is rejected. Existing WORKING_WEEK rows are preserved and used only when no new template/exception applies. They are never silently converted into a recurring schedule.

New authenticated routes under `/api/working-schedules/recurring`: GET `?dentistId&date` returns the employee revision token, effective template, exception for date and future changes; POST `/preview`, `/publish`, `/preview-availability`. Command: `{dentistId,date,kind:Template|Exception,expectedVersion,requestKey,intervals:[{branchId,day,startMinute,endMinute,kind,reason}]}`. Exception periods use the selected date's weekday. Missing weekday means Not working. Only one branch per weekday; all submitted branches require edit permission. Editing the employee-wide aggregate also requires edit permission for every active branch membership and previously published branch, preventing a manager from removing another branch's hours. Exception reason is required, including absence. GET includes legacyWeek for explicitly dated migration context; unsaved forms are never auto-filled from legacy hours.

Publication uses the existing ReadCommitted transaction and advisory lock 90403. The employee revision token increments on every new template or exception; duplicate actor/requestKey replays the exact original response, altered payload conflicts. Same-date publication replaces that effective revision while retaining older audit rows. New writes cannot take effect before the clinic current date. Preview and publication check every future Confirmed reservation assigned to the employee, across all branches and without limiting protection to the slot preview range. They apply later templates and dated exceptions before deciding impact. No confirmed appointment is moved. Draft slot preview accepts an inclusive date range up to 31 days, with branch/service/duration/assistant, and substitutes the proposed template or exception only where it would actually apply. Public preview reads published availability only. Booking uses the same resolver and employee revision token, so any published change invalidates stale booking versions. Legacy write endpoints reject writes for employees with new revisions, preventing conflicting schedule sources.

UI: employee defaults to self; choose Template or dated Exception, effective/exception date, and weekday branch or Not working. Exception reason, 30-minute time controls, optional extra spans and breaks, impact list and range slot preview are explicit. Not working removes only that day's draft intervals. Dirty navigation, reload and discard require confirmation. Validation, denied, stale and connection errors preserve the draft and release loading. Publication displays persisted read-back and actor/time; pending future templates/exceptions and legacy week context remain visible. Opening S09 from a future S52 employee profile may pass `dentistId`; the server still enforces authorization. S52 itself remains outside this change.

Required regression: recurring hours available in later weeks, weekday-specific branches, Not working, dated absence and extra work overriding one date only, later effective templates, all-future cross-branch appointment protection, authorization, stale/idempotent publication, rollback, legacy fallback and booking resource/version checks. UI verifies simple rows, 30-minute entry, drafts after errors, exceptions and preview range. The older sections below document legacy compatibility; they do not override this revision.

Owner: Tư Nguyễn Văn. Baseline: supplied Word pair v2.0 dated 15 October 2026, S09 / UC-03; GBR-001–008, GBR-081, GBR-102, GBR-107, GBR-119; MSG38/39/110/111/144. This contract refines the baseline for this iteration. TV2 owns appointment creation/reservation; TV4 consumes availability. No appointment command endpoint is added here.

## API contract

### Doctor appointment list — 7 November 2026

`GET /api/working-schedules/appointments?dentistId={employeeId}&from=yyyy-MM-dd&to=yyyy-MM-dd` returns `{items:[{id,code,branchId,branchName,startAt,endAt,patientName,serviceName,status,isTreatmentPlan,patientConsentConfirmedAt}]}`. Inclusive clinic-local dates, maximum 31 days; the UI requests the Monday–Sunday week containing its selected date. It shows all appointment statuses, including cancellation, so an old confirmed row does not remain after a change.

Requires `working-schedules.read` and the same employee visibility and active branch membership rules as schedule options. A dentist reads their own authorized branches; a schedule manager may read a doctor's appointments only in branches where the manager can view that doctor. Other employee IDs are rejected and assignment branch limits remain enforced. API responses omit phone/email, clinical notes and consent actor details. They do not grant appointment modification rights. The web links to appointment details only with `appointments.read`.

The working-schedules page adds a separate appointment card with week navigation/reload. Reading or moving through weeks does not edit recurring templates, exceptions or their dirty drafts. Focus refresh reads current saved appointments; confirmed patient agreement is shown from server metadata, legacy plan bookings show Not recorded. Consent capture is specified in `TREATMENT-NEXT-VISIT-BOOKING.md`.

All routes use `/api`. Dates are ISO local dates; weekStart must be Monday. Minutes are integers from midnight in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh, on a 15-minute grid, with end exclusive (1440 permitted). Instants in availability use explicit +07:00 offsets. Adjacent periods are valid; zero, reversed and overlapping work periods are invalid. Break/Block periods must lie wholly inside one Work period, cannot overlap each other and require a reason of 1–500 characters. A week can be empty, subject to booked-work protection.

- `GET /working-schedules/options`: authorized branch memberships, employee choices, chairs and services; Dentist defaults to self. Only active memberships/accounts are usable. Owner may edit branch members only where explicitly granted CanManageSchedules; other Dentists edit themselves. Admin role alone grants no clinical scheduling access.
- `GET /working-schedules?branchId&dentistId&weekStart`: authorized published week, ID (null if absent), version (0 if absent), status, intervals, actor/time. dentistId identifies EmployeeProfile, not AccountId. Employee schedules also cover assistant availability. No draft is persisted.
- `POST /working-schedules/preview`: body `{branchId,dentistId,weekStart,expectedVersion,intervals,requestKey}`; validates a proposed week and returns affected Confirmed appointments without writing. Preview exposes appointment code/time/attendee label/status only to authorized schedule editors. Resolve affected appointments in S12; S12 remains TV2-owned.
- `POST /working-schedules/publish`: same body; requestKey is a nonempty UUID, expectedVersion required (0 for initial publication). Success returns `{schedule,notification:MSG39}` including ID/version/Published/actor/time. Each successful command stores a receipt by actor/requestKey and payload fingerprint; exact retry returns its original result, different payload with the same key returns 409 MSG111. Validation returns 400 MSG110; stale returns 409 MSG111; affected appointments return 409 MSG38 and are obtained through preview. Failure never replaces the old published week. Discard is local and reloads the saved week.
- `GET /availability/options?branchId`: public active branch/dentist/service catalog without account IDs or internal schedules.
- `GET /availability?branchId&dentistId&serviceId&date&durationMinutes&assistantId?`: anonymous/public duration must equal the active publicBookable service duration (1–480); authenticated staff may choose integer minutes 1–480 and must belong to the branch. Public starts honor 60-minute lead time. Returns `{timeZone,date,slots:[{start,end,chairId,dentistId,assistantId,scheduleVersion,assistantScheduleVersion,bookingSettingsVersion}]}`. Starts use the branch slot interval. No attendee information or reservations are exposed. No slot is held. GET never accepts draft intervals.
- `POST /working-schedules/preview-availability`: authenticated editor supplies `{schedule,query}` with the same shared DTOs; computes slots against the proposed main schedule, after validation. Does not publish or hold anything.

UI states: loading, no published week, empty valid slots, validation error, connection failure, denied access and stale/resource conflict have separate messages. Errors preserve edited values. Explicit reload/discard asks before dropping unsaved edits. Week/branch/employee changes also confirm. Date selection defaults to the clinic current week. Published read-back replaces the form only after success. Affected appointments link to `/appointments/{id}` (staff appointment detail with permission-scoped S12 request handling).

Validation diagnostics: before impact preview, staff draft availability preview or publication, the web editor identifies invalid periods by their displayed interval numbers and weekdays. Explain reversed/non-grid times, overlapping Work periods, overlapping exclusions, missing/oversized reasons and exclusions outside one Work interval. Preserve the draft and show MSG110 with these concrete corrections; the server remains authoritative. Each row also displays its actual start/end in 24-hour HH:mm so native AM/PM controls cannot obscure the submitted time. Every newly added period defaults to Work; users must explicitly select Break or Block for exclusions. A valid day must not be rejected because its Work interval contains its own Break/Block periods.

Duration diagnostics: integer staff durations 1–480, including 35 and 70, are valid. Blank, fractional, out-of-range and non-finite durations are invalid; preserve the input/draft and release loading. Do not silently substitute 30 for blank input. Public preview takes the selected service's configured duration and remains read-only. Changing duration does not change the branch start grid.

## Resource contract for TV2 and TV4

Application `IAvailabilityService` exports `QueryAsync` and `CheckInTransactionAsync(ResourceCheck)`. TV2 must use the same scoped DbContext and open an explicit ReadCommitted transaction, call `CheckInTransactionAsync`, insert/update its reservation using the returned fixed chair/staff, then commit. The check requires a transaction and takes PostgreSQL transaction advisory lock 90403, also used by publication. All reservation writers and resource/block editors must acquire this lock before reading or mutating capacity. Global lock is a deliberate first-iteration correctness choice; fine-grained locking may replace it only with concurrent tests. GET takes no lock and gives no guarantee against later booking. TV2 must also implement its own requestKey/appointment validation.

`ResourceCheck` contains branch, main dentist, optional distinct assistant, service, start, duration, chair, identified attendee key, expected main schedule version, optional expected assistant schedule version, optional reservation ID to exclude when rescheduling, and public/staff mode. Internal callers supply an authorized attendee identity; public clients cannot query another attendee. Check rejects a caller without a transaction, stale schedules, non-grid times, inactive/wrong-branch resources, invalid roles, intervals cutting a break/block or work boundary, and overlaps involving any assigned employee, chair or identified attendee, including other branches. Half-open intervals permit adjacent bookings. Entire interval uses one chair and the same staff; no stitching across work periods. A Confirmed reservation is protected when publishing any assigned employee's week. Publication checks the whole booked interval and current resources, including assistant and chair; it excludes that reservation from occupancy checks.

Resource check failures use 409 MSG26 (insufficient consecutive published time), MSG27 (assigned staff or identified attendee collision), MSG28 (selected chair capacity), MSG25 (public lead time is no longer available); stale versions use MSG111. Structurally invalid requests use 400 MSG110. The caller transaction must be ReadCommitted; other isolation levels are rejected to prevent using a snapshot taken before the advisory lock. Public duration must match the selected service catalog duration.

Resource configuration: SchedulingBranch includes slot interval and booking settings version; services include integer duration and publicBookable. The booking settings UI edits these at an authorized branch. Existing branch/employee membership, published work/break/block periods and ResourceReservation capacity checks remain shared with booking. Confirmed/Checked-in reservations occupy capacity. Change 10 November 2026: a chair is only a capacity resource, not a time window. Working time comes exclusively from the published dentist/assistant schedule; a slot needs at least one active chair of the branch that is not occupied by an overlapping Confirmed/Checked-in reservation, so N active chairs allow up to N dentists to hold the same slot. Chair `OpenMinute`/`CloseMinute` are legacy columns, always stored as 0/1440 for new chairs and ignored by availability and booking checks; MSG28 now only means no free chair. Broader branch/chair/employee administration remains outside this page.

## Acceptance evidence

Reported sample regression (19 October 2026, Monday): Work 08:00–17:00, Break 12:00–13:00 with reason Nghỉ trưa, Block 15:00–15:30 with reason Họp nội bộ is a valid day. Preview and publication accept it. At 30 minutes, 08:00–08:30 and 16:30–17:00 are available with free fixtures; starts cutting either exclusion are absent. At 120 minutes, 10:00–12:00 is valid, 11:00–13:00 and 14:00–16:00 are excluded. UI regression must enter all three periods through the real editor, including changing the type of the second and third added periods from the default Work value.

Business tests cover clinic offset, Monday/date/grid boundaries, adjacency, 60/120-minute full spans, breaks and invalid overlaps, chair/staff/attendee collisions, branch/role/activity, protected Confirmed bookings, idempotency, concurrent publication and stale versions. API tests cover authorization and real PostgreSQL persistence. Web build and UI tests cover editing, preview, affected appointments, errors and discard. Migration and development fixtures are repeatable and do not overwrite existing accounts. Run instructions and actual check results belong in `docs/implementation/S09-UC03.md`.

## Bố cục lịch làm việc gọn theo hàng (09/11/2026)

Cùng ngày, bỏ khung thông báo "Có lịch tuần cũ được giữ lại" khỏi giao diện lịch làm việc. Vẫn giữ trạng thái công bố và quy tắc fallback lịch cũ ở backend; trường `legacyWeek` trong API không thay đổi.

Thay thế bố cục thẻ ngày ngày 08/11: trình biên tập hiển thị Thứ 2 đến Chủ nhật theo bảy hàng trong một khối, mỗi hàng có ngày, chi nhánh và các khoảng giờ. Ngày không làm việc dùng nền nhẹ và nhãn rõ, không có khung rỗng cao bằng ngày nhiều ca. Ngoại lệ chỉ hiển thị hàng của ngày đang chọn.

- Desktop: chi nhánh nằm cạnh ngày; mỗi khoảng giờ xếp loại ca, giờ bắt đầu/kết thúc, lý do và nút xóa trên cùng hàng khi đủ chỗ. Khoảng làm việc và nghỉ/block có dấu màu kèm nhãn chữ. Tránh lặp lại khoảng giờ phía trên các ô nhập; số khoảng vẫn hiện nhỏ để đối chiếu thông báo kiểm tra.
- Màn nhỏ: các phần tự xuống hàng, hai ô giờ nằm cạnh nhau, lý do chiếm hàng riêng; khối biên tập không gây cuộn ngang trang. Các ô nhập có nhãn và nút xóa có tên truy cập đầy đủ.
- Nút thêm khoảng đặt cuối danh sách ca của từng ngày; vẫn dùng giá trị mặc định hiện có. Nút xóa là icon nhỏ, có tooltip. Các điều khiển vẫn bị khóa theo quyền quản lý và trạng thái tải. Không thêm hành động tự động lưu/công bố.
- Giữ nguyên chọn chi nhánh hoặc Không làm việc, bước giờ 30 phút, loại Work/Break/Block, lý do, đánh số khoảng dùng chung với validation, khôi phục bản nháp, preview ảnh hưởng và công bố idempotent. Không thay API, quy tắc phân quyền hoặc dữ liệu lịch đã lưu. Thẻ lịch hẹn tuần tiếp tục độc lập với bản nháp.

Kiểm chứng: chạy lại kiểm thử trình biên tập hiện có (chọn ngày/chi nhánh, thêm ca, đổi giờ, ngoại lệ, lỗi/retry, preview và công bố); xem ảnh desktop và màn 390px có đủ ca làm/nghỉ/ngày nghỉ, kiểm tra không tràn ngang trong khối chỉnh lịch. Kiểm tra giao diện thật bằng đăng nhập mà không công bố thay đổi lên lịch đang dùng.
