Bảng use case DCMS (bản gộp, 46 UC)

Mỗi dòng là một mục tiêu của một actor. Các dòng "Xem…" và CRUD danh mục đã được gộp vào use case cha.

Actor: Guest, Patient (tài khoản khách hàng), Clinic Assistant, Dentist, Clinic Owner, Admin, System (tác vụ nền).

Quy ước tên: "Manage X" chỉ dùng cho quản lý danh mục/đối tượng với các thao tác cùng loại (thêm, sửa, tìm, ẩn hoặc hiện). Quy trình nghiệp vụ dùng động từ cụ thể (Book, Reschedule, Plan, Record, Track...).

Nội dung dựa trên code hiện tại (backend `Features`, web, mobile). Các điểm đã chỉnh so với bản cũ: thu tiền chỉ Clinic Owner; tạo/sửa tài khoản nhân viên chỉ Admin; Dentist chỉ xem hồ sơ bệnh nhân; không có xóa tài liệu lâm sàng.

UC-02Sign In with GoogleAccount and AccessPatientAuthenticate a patient with a Google account when Google sign-in is configured on the server.
UC-03Register Patient AccountAccount and AccessGuest, PatientCreate a patient account with a name, a Vietnamese mobile number, an e-mail and a password that follows the password policy (web and mobile).

UC-35Order Lab WorkTreatmentDentist, Clinic OwnerCreate, edit or remove a lab order for a piece of work on a treatment sheet.

UC-40Manage Clinic CatalogsAdministrationClinic Owner, DentistAdd, edit, search and deactivate or reactivate treatment services and prices, diagnoses, medicines and customer groups, with export where available.
UC-41Manage Supply CatalogAdministrationClinic OwnerAdd, edit, search and deactivate or delete material categories, instruments and materials, suppliers, lab companies and types of lab work.

UC-43Manage Branches and Dental ChairsAdministrationClinic OwnerCreate and edit a branch, activate or deactivate it after checking the effect, and add, edit, activate or deactivate its chairs.
UC-44Manage General SettingsAdministrationClinic OwnerSet the staff idle sign-out time, the required patient fields, the patient code mode and prefix and the receipt print options, with preview, version history and restore; see who changed which setting.
UC-45View Finance StatisticsReportsClinic OwnerSee the amount to collect, the money received, the advance, the receivables, the cost of materials and the profit, and open the list behind each figure.
UC-46View Work and Service StatisticsReportsClinic OwnerSee the work and services of treatment sheets with the cost, the paid and the remaining amount, filtered by branch, period, status and search.
