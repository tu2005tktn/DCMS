# DCMS business rules (from code)

Business (domain) rules actually enforced by the current backend (`backend/src`), mapped to the refined use cases UC-01 to UC-46. Generated 22 November 2026 from the services, domain rules and endpoints; the older v2.5 GBR list in `DCMS_Use_Cases_Business_Rules_Messages_v2.5.docx` contains rules that are no longer, or not yet, implemented. Technical rules (tokens, field lengths, request keys, version checks, file hashing, rate limits) are not listed.

| GBR-ID | Rule Statement | Applies To | Rationale | Enforced By |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GBR-001 | **Account usability**<br>A locked or deactivated account cannot use the system. Locking a staff account ends their current work sessions at once. | UC-01, UC-05 | Staff who leave or are suspended lose access immediately. | Admin; system |
| GBR-002 | **Patient account registration**<br>A patient account requires a full name, a Vietnamese mobile number and an e-mail; the phone number is used to sign in. One phone number and one e-mail belong to only one account. A new account can book appointments but sees no medical record until it is linked. | UC-03, UC-10 | Each patient account is unique and reachable. | System registration |
| GBR-003 | **Google sign-in for patients**<br>Google sign-in is offered only to patients. If the Google e-mail already belongs to an account registered another way, the patient must sign in with that method. | UC-02 | One person keeps one patient account. | System |
| GBR-004 | **Account recovery**<br>A user who forgot the password must prove their identity with a code sent to the registered contact before setting a new password. An Admin can start the recovery for a staff account. Resetting the password does not unlock a locked account. | UC-04, UC-05 | Only the account owner regains access. | Patient, staff; Admin; system |
| GBR-005 | **Staff account**<br>Each staff account belongs to one employee, with a unique employee code, user name, e-mail and phone number. Locking or unlocking a staff account requires a reason, and every change is kept in the account history. | UC-05 | Each action can be traced to one employee. | Admin |
| GBR-006 | **Access groups**<br>An access group is based on one role (Admin, Dentist or Clinic Assistant) and lists the screens and actions it may use; a detailed action requires its parent screen. A person can only grant rights they hold and cannot change their own group. A group with staff assigned cannot be deactivated or change its role. | UC-06 | No one can raise their own rights. | Clinic Owner, Admin |
| GBR-007 | **Branch scope**<br>Staff work only with the branches they are assigned to. A dentist without wider rights sees only their own appointments. | UC-05, UC-06, UC-15, UC-23, UC-24 | Each branch's patients are handled by its own staff. | System |
| GBR-008 | **Appointment reminders**<br>The patient is reminded 24 hours and 2 hours before a confirmed appointment; a reminder whose time has passed is not sent. After a reschedule, reminders follow the new time; after a cancellation they stop. | UC-07, UC-08, UC-09, UC-17 | Reduce missed appointments. | System |
| GBR-009 | **Notification failure**<br>If a notification cannot be delivered, the appointment action still stands and the clinic staff receive an item to contact the patient themselves. | UC-07 | Appointments never depend on messaging. | System; Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-010 | **Booking lead time**<br>An online booking must start at least 60 minutes from now. Staff may book closer to the start only with a reason. No appointment can be booked in the past. | UC-08, UC-16 | Give the clinic time to prepare. | System |
| GBR-011 | **Online bookable service**<br>Patients can book online only the branch's initial examination service, with the duration set by the clinic (30 minutes by default); they cannot choose a duration. Treatment visits are booked by the clinic. | UC-08, UC-16, UC-22 | Keep online bookings simple and correctly timed. | Clinic Owner configuration; system |
| GBR-012 | **Slot boundaries**<br>Appointment start times are offered only within the dentist's published working time, at the branch slot interval. The whole appointment must fit in working time without touching a break or blocked time. | UC-08, UC-16, UC-17, UC-21 | Keep appointments within available working time. | System slot generation |
| GBR-013 | **No double booking**<br>A dentist (and assistant) cannot have two appointments at the same time, and the same patient cannot have two overlapping appointments. Each appointment needs a free dental chair of the branch. | UC-08, UC-16, UC-17 | People and chairs are never double-booked. | System availability check |
| GBR-014 | **Any available dentist**<br>When the patient chooses "any dentist", each time is given to the free dentist on duty with the fewest appointments that day. | UC-08, UC-16 | Spread the workload fairly. | System |
| GBR-015 | **Who is booked**<br>A booking records the booker with at least a phone number or an e-mail, and the person who will actually attend. A patient can book for themselves (using their linked record) or for a family member saved in their own account only. | UC-08, UC-13 | The clinic knows who comes and who to contact. | System |
| GBR-016 | **Automatic confirmation**<br>A valid booking is confirmed immediately; there is no waiting for staff approval. | UC-08, UC-16 | Patients get an immediate answer. | System |
| GBR-017 | **Self-service change window**<br>A patient can reschedule or cancel their own confirmed appointment only while at least 12 hours remain. Closer to the appointment they must send a support request to the clinic. | UC-09 | Give the clinic time to reuse the slot. | System |
| GBR-018 | **Rescheduling**<br>The new time must be at least 60 minutes ahead and actually available. If the change fails, the original appointment is kept. A reason is required and every change is kept in the appointment history. | UC-09, UC-17, UC-20 | A failed change never loses the booking. | Patient, Clinic Assistant, Dentist; system |
| GBR-019 | **Cancellation**<br>Cancelling requires a reason and frees the time slot. Staff can cancel a confirmed appointment any time before it starts. | UC-09, UC-18 | Keep a clear record of cancellations. | Patient, Clinic Assistant, Dentist |
| GBR-020 | **Support request**<br>A support request can be sent only for a confirmed appointment that has not started, with a reason; only one request can be open per appointment. The appointment stays valid until the clinic handles the request. The patient can withdraw a request that is still pending. | UC-09, UC-20 | Late changes go through the clinic. | Patient; system |
| GBR-021 | **Handling support requests**<br>Before applying a change or cancellation, the assistant must record how they verified the requester. A request is resolved only after the change is actually made; a rejection needs a reason. | UC-20 | Only the real requester can change an appointment. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-022 | **Link to own record**<br>Only a patient account can ask to link itself to a patient record, and must confirm it is their own record. An account can have only one linked own record, and a record only one linked account. | UC-10 | Medical data belongs to one person. | Patient; system |
| GBR-023 | **Verify a link**<br>A link can be approved only after the patient's first visit (when the record exists) and when the full name, and the birth date and patient code if given, match the record. The assistant records the verification and can revoke a link later. | UC-10, UC-25 | Medical records are shown only to the right person. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-024 | **Access to own medical record**<br>A patient sees their medical record and can update their information only after the link is verified. | UC-11, UC-12 | Protect medical records. | System |
| GBR-025 | **Update own information**<br>A patient can change only administrative information (name, birth date, gender, phone, address, guardian) of their linked record; clinical data and internal notes cannot be changed. | UC-12 | Patients keep their contact details current. | System |
| GBR-026 | **Family members**<br>Family members are saved in, and visible only to, the account that added them. Removing a family member only hides them from the list; their past appointments are kept. | UC-13 | Relatives' data stays private. | Patient |
| GBR-027 | **Family record access**<br>A patient sees a relative's record only after the clinic grants it with the relative's consent (or as legal representative), for chosen parts of the record (examinations, treatment, prescriptions, documents) and for at most 365 days. The patient can withdraw the request and the clinic can revoke it. | UC-14, UC-25 | Share a relative's record only with consent and limits. | Clinic Assistant; system |
| GBR-028 | **Clinic booking**<br>Clinic assistants book standard and walk-in appointments for their branch and record the source (phone, Zalo, Facebook, in person, app). A dentist books only follow-up visits of their own treatment plans. Walk-in appointments are created only by staff. | UC-16, UC-19 | Bookings come from the right role and channel. | Clinic Assistant, Dentist |
| GBR-029 | **No-show**<br>Only staff can mark a No-show, only after the appointment time has started and only if the patient has not checked in. A reason is required. | UC-18 | Never mark a patient who actually arrived. | Clinic Assistant, Dentist |
| GBR-030 | **Walk-in in a freed slot**<br>The time of a No-show appointment can be given to a walk-in patient while it is still running, with the same dentist and ending within that time. | UC-18 | Reuse chair time that would be lost. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-031 | **Next visit**<br>A next visit is booked only for an open treatment sheet of the same patient and branch, with the patient's consent. Suggested dates are within 4 days before or after the planned date of the open plan item. | UC-19, UC-31 | Visits follow the agreed treatment plan. | Clinic Assistant, Dentist |
| GBR-032 | **Follow-up after a no-show**<br>Every No-show of a known patient creates one care task to contact the patient. | UC-18, UC-26 | Every missed patient is followed up. | System |
| GBR-033 | **Care tasks**<br>A care task is Open, In progress, Done or Cancelled; a finished or cancelled task can no longer change. Each contact attempt records the channel and the result (reached, no answer, rebooked, declined, other). | UC-26 | Keep a reliable contact history. | Clinic Assistant, Dentist |
| GBR-034 | **Working schedule**<br>A dentist's schedule consists of work periods, breaks and blocked time in 15-minute steps. A break or block must be inside a work period and needs a reason; periods of the same kind cannot overlap. | UC-21 | Avoid conflicting published work intervals. | Clinic Owner, Dentist |
| GBR-035 | **Weekly template and exceptions**<br>The weekly template repeats every week; an exception changes one day only and needs a reason. | UC-21 | Handle one-off changes without rewriting the week. | Clinic Owner, Dentist |
| GBR-036 | **Protect confirmed appointments**<br>A schedule change that would leave confirmed appointments outside working time cannot be published until those appointments are moved or cancelled. | UC-21 | Booked patients are never left without a dentist. | System |
| GBR-037 | **Appointment settings**<br>Each branch sets its slot interval (15 minutes by default) and which services can be booked online with their duration. A branch always keeps an initial examination service. | UC-22 | Online booking always has a service to offer. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-038 | **Check-in**<br>Only a confirmed appointment of today that has not ended can be checked in; a No-show cannot. The patient must match the appointment (linked record, or name and birth date) and the record must be active. One appointment gives one visit. | UC-23 | The right patient is attached to the right visit. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-039 | **Arrival time**<br>The arrival time is today and not in the future; recording an earlier time than now needs permission and a reason. | UC-23 | Queue times stay truthful. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-040 | **Required patient information**<br>The clinic chooses which patient fields are required. A newly required field applies from the next time a record is saved; existing records are not changed. | UC-24, UC-44 | Collect the information the clinic needs. | Clinic Owner configuration |
| GBR-041 | **Duplicate patients**<br>Before creating or editing a record, possible duplicates (same name, same phone, or same birth date and given name) are shown and must be reviewed. Records are never merged automatically. | UC-23, UC-24 | One patient, one record. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-042 | **Patient code**<br>Each patient gets a unique code, generated automatically, entered by hand, or both, as the clinic chooses. A code never changes once given, and a new code mode applies only to new patients. | UC-24, UC-44 | Each patient has one stable code. | System; Clinic Owner configuration |
| GBR-043 | **Archived patient**<br>An archived record cannot be edited, checked in or prescribed for until it is restored. | UC-23, UC-24, UC-38 | Inactive records stay unchanged. | Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-044 | **Examination flow**<br>A dentist starts an examination only for a checked-in patient in the queue. A visit created to continue treatment is recorded on the treatment sheet, not as a new examination. | UC-27 | Keep examination and treatment separate. | Dentist |
| GBR-045 | **Confirm examination**<br>An examination can be confirmed only with at least one diagnosis and a decision on what happens next; diagnoses no longer in use cannot be chosen. | UC-27, UC-40 | Every examination ends with a clinical conclusion. | Dentist |
| GBR-046 | **Complete and hand over**<br>A visit is completed only after the dentist has reviewed the work and every investigation needed for the diagnosis is read or cancelled. The patient is handed over to treatment only when the examination decided to proceed to treatment. | UC-27, UC-31 | No visit closes with unread results. | Dentist |
| GBR-047 | **Amend examination**<br>A confirmed or completed examination is changed only by an amendment with a reason; the earlier version is kept. | UC-27 | Clinical records stay traceable. | Dentist |
| GBR-048 | **Investigations**<br>An investigation is ordered, its result received, then reviewed by the dentist; it can be cancelled before review. Ordering one after the examination is confirmed needs a reason. | UC-28 | Results are read before decisions. | Dentist, Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-049 | **Examination documents**<br>Images and documents (X-ray, intraoral photo, lab result, consent form, referral, other) are added to an open visit or an ordered investigation. Removing one requires a reason; investigation results cannot be removed. | UC-28, UC-29 | Clinical evidence is kept. | Dentist, Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-050 | **AI X-ray analysis**<br>AI analysis is available only for X-ray images and only when the clinic has the AI service. Its result is a suggestion shown to the dentist and kept with the image; it never replaces the dentist's diagnosis. | UC-30 | AI supports, not replaces, the dentist. | Dentist |
| GBR-051 | **Open sheet required**<br>Plan items, treatment work and lab orders can be added or changed only while the treatment sheet is open. | UC-31, UC-32, UC-35 | Closed sheets stay unchanged. | System |
| GBR-052 | **Quantity and teeth**<br>Units counted per tooth, jaw, time or package take whole numbers. Teeth use FDI numbering. The work actually done must stay within the teeth agreed in the plan. | UC-31, UC-32, UC-34 | Work matches what the patient agreed. | Dentist |
| GBR-053 | **Price and discount**<br>Prices are never negative. The amount of an item is quantity x agreed price. A discount is a fixed amount or a percentage (at most 100%), never more than the item amount, and requires a reason. | UC-31, UC-32, UC-40 | Agreed prices and discounts are justified. | Dentist, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-054 | **Close a treatment sheet**<br>A sheet closes as completed as agreed, completed with a revised plan, or stopped before completion; the last two need a reason. Upcoming follow-up appointments of the sheet must be handled first. | UC-33 | Each sheet ends with a clear outcome. | Dentist, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-055 | **Reopen a treatment sheet**<br>A completed or stopped sheet can be reopened with a reason; a cancelled sheet cannot. | UC-33 | Reopening is deliberate. | Dentist, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-056 | **Delete or cancel a sheet**<br>A sheet can be deleted only when it was opened by mistake and has nothing recorded (no plan, work, appointments, documents or payments). Otherwise it is cancelled with a reason, which is not possible while a bank-transfer QR is still unsettled. | UC-33, UC-39 | Never delete clinical or financial history. | Dentist, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-057 | **Treatment plan templates**<br>A branch keeps ready-made plans whose items use valid services, teeth and staff. Items that are no longer valid cannot be copied into a treatment sheet. | UC-34, UC-31 | Templates produce valid plans. | Dentist, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-058 | **Lab orders**<br>A lab order belongs to a piece of work on a treatment sheet, goes to an active lab company, and its due and delivery dates cannot be before the order date. Once the patient has paid toward it, it cannot be removed and its fee cannot go below the amount paid. | UC-35, UC-39 | Lab work and its payments stay consistent. | Dentist, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-059 | **Lab order progress**<br>A lab order moves Not sent, Sent, Received, Delivered; it can go back to Rework from Sent, Received or Delivered and is then sent again. Every move is kept in its history. | UC-36 | Track lab work through a fixed process. | Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner |
| GBR-060 | **Materials used**<br>Materials recorded for a piece of work are taken out of stock and cannot exceed the stock on hand. | UC-37, UC-42 | Stock reflects real use. | Dentist, Clinic Assistant; system |
| GBR-061 | **Prescription**<br>Only a dentist of the branch prescribes, for an active patient, with up to 30 medicines from clinic stock or bought outside. Medicines are counted in whole base units. | UC-38 | Prescriptions come from authorized dentists. | Dentist |
| GBR-062 | **Who handles money**<br>Only the Clinic Owner records payments, advances and their use, and sees the finance figures (unless an access group grants it). | UC-39, UC-45 | One accountable role handles money. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-063 | **Payment**<br>A payment cannot be dated in the future and cannot exceed what remains to be paid for each service. | UC-39 | No overpayment. | Clinic Owner; system |
| GBR-064 | **Lab fee first**<br>Before other services on a sheet are paid, at least 30% of each lab fee must be paid; an advance is used for that lab part first. | UC-39, UC-35 | The clinic covers its lab cost first. | System |
| GBR-065 | **Using an advance**<br>Money taken from an advance cannot exceed the advance balance. | UC-39 | Only money actually received is used. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-066 | **Forfeit a no-show advance**<br>The advance of a patient who did not come can be forfeited only when the sheet has no treatment work or lab order and the advance has not been used; a reason is required. | UC-39, UC-18 | Forfeit only money that was never used. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-067 | **PayOS payment**<br>A bank-transfer QR is valid for 30 minutes and only one can be active per sheet and purpose. The payment is recorded automatically when PayOS confirms it. | UC-39 | Online payments are recorded only when really received. | System |
| GBR-068 | **Unique catalog entries**<br>Each catalog entry (service, diagnosis, medicine, customer group, material, supplier, lab company, lab work type) has a unique code and name. | UC-40, UC-41 | Entries are not confused with each other. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-069 | **Deactivate instead of delete**<br>An entry already used is deactivated, not deleted, and needs a reason; entries used by the system cannot be deactivated. Inactive entries cannot be chosen for new work. A material category that still has items cannot be deleted. | UC-40, UC-41 | Past records keep their catalog data. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-070 | **Units**<br>Quantities are converted to the base unit exactly: materials may have up to 3 decimals, medicines must be whole units. | UC-37, UC-38, UC-41, UC-42 | Stock and doses stay exact. | System |
| GBR-071 | **Purchases**<br>A purchase cannot be entered for a future date; the received date is between the entry date and today; the expiry date is not before the entry date; the amount paid is not above the total. It uses an active material and an active supplier that is not a lab company. | UC-42 | Purchases reflect real deliveries. | Clinic Owner, Clinic Assistant |
| GBR-072 | **Stock balance**<br>Stock on hand is the quantity received minus the quantity used and can never be negative. | UC-42, UC-37 | Stock shows real quantities. | System |
| GBR-073 | **Deactivate a branch**<br>A branch cannot be deactivated while it has future appointments, waiting or ongoing visits, open treatment sheets or lab orders not delivered. The impact is shown first, a reason is required, and nothing is cancelled automatically. | UC-43 | Patients' open matters are never abandoned. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-074 | **Dental chairs**<br>A chair can be activated only in an active branch and cannot be deactivated while it still has obligations such as booked appointments. | UC-43 | Booked chairs stay available. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-075 | **General settings history**<br>Every settings change needs a reason and creates a new version; an older version can be restored as a new version. Changes apply to new records and the next printout, not to existing ones. | UC-44 | Settings changes are traceable and reversible. | Clinic Owner |
| GBR-076 | **Finance figures**<br>Deleted sheets are not counted. A cancelled sheet keeps the money already collected and has no amount left to collect. Profit counts only fully paid sheets (revenue minus lab cost), minus the cost of material purchases. | UC-45 | Figures reflect money actually earned. | System |
| GBR-077 | **Work and service figures**<br>The cost of each work line is quantity x price minus discount; paid and remaining amounts follow the payments of the sheet. Deleted sheets and deleted work are not counted. | UC-46 | Statistics match the treatment sheets. | System |
