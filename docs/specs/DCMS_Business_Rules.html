GBR-IDRule StatementApplies ToRationaleEnforced By
GBR-001Account usability
A locked or deactivated account cannot use the system. Locking a staff account ends their current work sessions at once.		UC-01, UC-05Staff who leave or are suspended lose access immediately.Admin; system
GBR-002Patient account registration
A patient account requires a full name, a Vietnamese mobile number and an e-mail; the phone number is used to sign in. One phone number and one e-mail belong to only one account. A new account can book appointments but sees no medical record until it is linked.		UC-03, UC-10Each patient account is unique and reachable.System registration
GBR-003Google sign-in for patients
Google sign-in is offered only to patients. If the Google e-mail already belongs to an account registered another way, the patient must sign in with that method.		UC-02One person keeps one patient account.System
GBR-004Account recovery
A user who forgot the password must prove their identity with a code sent to the registered contact before setting a new password. An Admin can start the recovery for a staff account. Resetting the password does not unlock a locked account.		UC-04, UC-05Only the account owner regains access.Patient, staff; Admin; system
GBR-005Staff account
Each staff account belongs to one employee, with a unique employee code, user name, e-mail and phone number. Locking or unlocking a staff account requires a reason, and every change is kept in the account history.		UC-05Each action can be traced to one employee.Admin
GBR-006Access groups
An access group is based on one role (Admin, Dentist or Clinic Assistant) and lists the screens and actions it may use; a detailed action requires its parent screen. A person can only grant rights they hold and cannot change their own group. A group with staff assigned cannot be deactivated or change its role.		UC-06No one can raise their own rights.Clinic Owner, Admin
GBR-007Branch scope
Staff work only with the branches they are assigned to. A dentist without wider rights sees only their own appointments.		UC-05, UC-06, UC-15, UC-23, UC-24Each branch's patients are handled by its own staff.System
GBR-008Appointment reminders
The patient is reminded 24 hours and 2 hours before a confirmed appointment; a reminder whose time has passed is not sent. After a reschedule, reminders follow the new time; after a cancellation they stop.		UC-07, UC-08, UC-09, UC-17Reduce missed appointments.System
GBR-009Notification failure
If a notification cannot be delivered, the appointment action still stands and the clinic staff receive an item to contact the patient themselves.		UC-07Appointments never depend on messaging.System; Clinic Assistant
GBR-010Booking lead time
An online booking must start at least 60 minutes from now. Staff may book closer to the start only with a reason. No appointment can be booked in the past.		UC-08, UC-16Give the clinic time to prepare.System
GBR-011Online bookable service
Patients can book online only the branch's initial examination service, with the duration set by the clinic (30 minutes by default); they cannot choose a duration. Treatment visits are booked by the clinic.		UC-08, UC-16, UC-22Keep online bookings simple and correctly timed.Clinic Owner configuration; system
GBR-012Slot boundaries
Appointment start times are offered only within the dentist's published working time, at the branch slot interval. The whole appointment must fit in working time without touching a break or blocked time.		UC-08, UC-16, UC-17, UC-21Keep appointments within available working time.System slot generation
GBR-013No double booking
A dentist (and assistant) cannot have two appointments at the same time, and the same patient cannot have two overlapping appointments. Each appointment needs a free dental chair of the branch.		UC-08, UC-16, UC-17People and chairs are never double-booked.System availability check
GBR-014Any available dentist
When the patient chooses "any dentist", each time is given to the free dentist on duty with the fewest appointments that day.		UC-08, UC-16Spread the workload fairly.System
GBR-015Who is booked
A booking records the booker with at least a phone number or an e-mail, and the person who will actually attend. A patient can book for themselves (using their linked record) or for a family member saved in their own account only.		UC-08, UC-13The clinic knows who comes and who to contact.System
GBR-016Automatic confirmation
A valid booking is confirmed immediately; there is no waiting for staff approval.		UC-08, UC-16Patients get an immediate answer.System
GBR-017Self-service change window
A patient can reschedule or cancel their own confirmed appointment only while at least 12 hours remain. Closer to the appointment they must send a support request to the clinic.		UC-09Give the clinic time to reuse the slot.System
GBR-018Rescheduling
The new time must be at least 60 minutes ahead and actually available. If the change fails, the original appointment is kept. A reason is required and every change is kept in the appointment history.		UC-09, UC-17, UC-20A failed change never loses the booking.Patient, Clinic Assistant, Dentist; system
GBR-019Cancellation
Cancelling requires a reason and frees the time slot. Staff can cancel a confirmed appointment any time before it starts.		UC-09, UC-18Keep a clear record of cancellations.Patient, Clinic Assistant, Dentist
GBR-020Support request
A support request can be sent only for a confirmed appointment that has not started, with a reason; only one request can be open per appointment. The appointment stays valid until the clinic handles the request. The patient can withdraw a request that is still pending.		UC-09, UC-20Late changes go through the clinic.Patient; system
GBR-021Handling support requests
Before applying a change or cancellation, the assistant must record how they verified the requester. A request is resolved only after the change is actually made; a rejection needs a reason.		UC-20Only the real requester can change an appointment.Clinic Assistant
GBR-022Link to own record
Only a patient account can ask to link itself to a patient record, and must confirm it is their own record. An account can have only one linked own record, and a record only one linked account.		UC-10Medical data belongs to one person.Patient; system
GBR-023Verify a link
A link can be approved only after the patient's first visit (when the record exists) and when the full name, and the birth date and patient code if given, match the record. The assistant records the verification and can revoke a link later.		UC-10, UC-25Medical records are shown only to the right person.Clinic Assistant
GBR-024Access to own medical record
A patient sees their medical record and can update their information only after the link is verified.		UC-11, UC-12Protect medical records.System
GBR-025Update own information
A patient can change only administrative information (name, birth date, gender, phone, address, guardian) of their linked record; clinical data and internal notes cannot be changed.		UC-12Patients keep their contact details current.System
GBR-026Family members
Family members are saved in, and visible only to, the account that added them. Removing a family member only hides them from the list; their past appointments are kept.		UC-13Relatives' data stays private.Patient
GBR-027Family record access
A patient sees a relative's record only after the clinic grants it with the relative's consent (or as legal representative), for chosen parts of the record (examinations, treatment, prescriptions, documents) and for at most 365 days. The patient can withdraw the request and the clinic can revoke it.		UC-14, UC-25Share a relative's record only with consent and limits.Clinic Assistant; system
GBR-028Clinic booking
Clinic assistants book standard and walk-in appointments for their branch and record the source (phone, Zalo, Facebook, in person, app). A dentist books only follow-up visits of their own treatment plans. Walk-in appointments are created only by staff.		UC-16, UC-19Bookings come from the right role and channel.Clinic Assistant, Dentist
GBR-029No-show
Only staff can mark a No-show, only after the appointment time has started and only if the patient has not checked in. A reason is required.		UC-18Never mark a patient who actually arrived.Clinic Assistant, Dentist
GBR-030Walk-in in a freed slot
The time of a No-show appointment can be given to a walk-in patient while it is still running, with the same dentist and ending within that time.		UC-18Reuse chair time that would be lost.Clinic Assistant
GBR-031Next visit
A next visit is booked only for an open treatment sheet of the same patient and branch, with the patient's consent. Suggested dates are within 4 days before or after the planned date of the open plan item.		UC-19, UC-31Visits follow the agreed treatment plan.Clinic Assistant, Dentist
GBR-032Follow-up after a no-show
Every No-show of a known patient creates one care task to contact the patient.		UC-18, UC-26Every missed patient is followed up.System
GBR-033Care tasks
A care task is Open, In progress, Done or Cancelled; a finished or cancelled task can no longer change. Each contact attempt records the channel and the result (reached, no answer, rebooked, declined, other).		UC-26Keep a reliable contact history.Clinic Assistant, Dentist
GBR-034Working schedule
A dentist's schedule consists of work periods, breaks and blocked time in 15-minute steps. A break or block must be inside a work period and needs a reason; periods of the same kind cannot overlap.		UC-21Avoid conflicting published work intervals.Clinic Owner, Dentist
GBR-035Weekly template and exceptions
The weekly template repeats every week; an exception changes one day only and needs a reason.		UC-21Handle one-off changes without rewriting the week.Clinic Owner, Dentist
GBR-036Protect confirmed appointments
A schedule change that would leave confirmed appointments outside working time cannot be published until those appointments are moved or cancelled.		UC-21Booked patients are never left without a dentist.System
GBR-037Appointment settings
Each branch sets its slot interval (15 minutes by default) and which services can be booked online with their duration. A branch always keeps an initial examination service.		UC-22Online booking always has a service to offer.Clinic Owner
GBR-038Check-in
Only a confirmed appointment of today that has not ended can be checked in; a No-show cannot. The patient must match the appointment (linked record, or name and birth date) and the record must be active. One appointment gives one visit.		UC-23The right patient is attached to the right visit.Clinic Assistant
GBR-039Arrival time
The arrival time is today and not in the future; recording an earlier time than now needs permission and a reason.		UC-23Queue times stay truthful.Clinic Assistant
GBR-040Required patient information
The clinic chooses which patient fields are required. A newly required field applies from the next time a record is saved; existing records are not changed.		UC-24, UC-44Collect the information the clinic needs.Clinic Owner configuration
GBR-041Duplicate patients
Before creating or editing a record, possible duplicates (same name, same phone, or same birth date and given name) are shown and must be reviewed. Records are never merged automatically.		UC-23, UC-24One patient, one record.Clinic Assistant
GBR-042Patient code
Each patient gets a unique code, generated automatically, entered by hand, or both, as the clinic chooses. A code never changes once given, and a new code mode applies only to new patients.		UC-24, UC-44Each patient has one stable code.System; Clinic Owner configuration
GBR-043Archived patient
An archived record cannot be edited, checked in or prescribed for until it is restored.		UC-23, UC-24, UC-38Inactive records stay unchanged.Clinic Assistant
GBR-044Examination flow
A dentist starts an examination only for a checked-in patient in the queue. A visit created to continue treatment is recorded on the treatment sheet, not as a new examination.		UC-27Keep examination and treatment separate.Dentist
GBR-045Confirm examination
An examination can be confirmed only with at least one diagnosis and a decision on what happens next; diagnoses no longer in use cannot be chosen.		UC-27, UC-40Every examination ends with a clinical conclusion.Dentist
GBR-046Complete and hand over
A visit is completed only after the dentist has reviewed the work and every investigation needed for the diagnosis is read or cancelled. The patient is handed over to treatment only when the examination decided to proceed to treatment.		UC-27, UC-31No visit closes with unread results.Dentist
GBR-047Amend examination
A confirmed or completed examination is changed only by an amendment with a reason; the earlier version is kept.		UC-27Clinical records stay traceable.Dentist
GBR-048Investigations
An investigation is ordered, its result received, then reviewed by the dentist; it can be cancelled before review. Ordering one after the examination is confirmed needs a reason.		UC-28Results are read before decisions.Dentist, Clinic Assistant
GBR-049Examination documents
Images and documents (X-ray, intraoral photo, lab result, consent form, referral, other) are added to an open visit or an ordered investigation. Removing one requires a reason; investigation results cannot be removed.		UC-28, UC-29Clinical evidence is kept.Dentist, Clinic Assistant
GBR-050AI X-ray analysis
AI analysis is available only for X-ray images and only when the clinic has the AI service. Its result is a suggestion shown to the dentist and kept with the image; it never replaces the dentist's diagnosis.		UC-30AI supports, not replaces, the dentist.Dentist
GBR-051Open sheet required
Plan items, treatment work and lab orders can be added or changed only while the treatment sheet is open.		UC-31, UC-32, UC-35Closed sheets stay unchanged.System
GBR-052Quantity and teeth
Units counted per tooth, jaw, time or package take whole numbers. Teeth use FDI numbering. The work actually done must stay within the teeth agreed in the plan.		UC-31, UC-32, UC-34Work matches what the patient agreed.Dentist
GBR-053Price and discount
Prices are never negative. The amount of an item is quantity x agreed price. A discount is a fixed amount or a percentage (at most 100%), never more than the item amount, and requires a reason.		UC-31, UC-32, UC-40Agreed prices and discounts are justified.Dentist, Clinic Owner
GBR-054Close a treatment sheet
A sheet closes as completed as agreed, completed with a revised plan, or stopped before completion; the last two need a reason. Upcoming follow-up appointments of the sheet must be handled first.		UC-33Each sheet ends with a clear outcome.Dentist, Clinic Owner
GBR-055Reopen a treatment sheet
A completed or stopped sheet can be reopened with a reason; a cancelled sheet cannot.		UC-33Reopening is deliberate.Dentist, Clinic Owner
GBR-056Delete or cancel a sheet
A sheet can be deleted only when it was opened by mistake and has nothing recorded (no plan, work, appointments, documents or payments). Otherwise it is cancelled with a reason, which is not possible while a bank-transfer QR is still unsettled.		UC-33, UC-39Never delete clinical or financial history.Dentist, Clinic Owner
GBR-057Treatment plan templates
A branch keeps ready-made plans whose items use valid services, teeth and staff. Items that are no longer valid cannot be copied into a treatment sheet.		UC-34, UC-31Templates produce valid plans.Dentist, Clinic Owner
GBR-058Lab orders
A lab order belongs to a piece of work on a treatment sheet, goes to an active lab company, and its due and delivery dates cannot be before the order date. Once the patient has paid toward it, it cannot be removed and its fee cannot go below the amount paid.		UC-35, UC-39Lab work and its payments stay consistent.Dentist, Clinic Owner
GBR-059Lab order progress
A lab order moves Not sent, Sent, Received, Delivered; it can go back to Rework from Sent, Received or Delivered and is then sent again. Every move is kept in its history.		UC-36Track lab work through a fixed process.Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner
GBR-060Materials used
Materials recorded for a piece of work are taken out of stock and cannot exceed the stock on hand.		UC-37, UC-42Stock reflects real use.Dentist, Clinic Assistant; system
GBR-061Prescription
Only a dentist of the branch prescribes, for an active patient, up to 30 medicines per prescription. Medicines are bought by the patient outside the clinic; the system does not deduct clinic medicine stock.		UC-38Prescriptions come from authorized dentists.Dentist
GBR-062Who handles money
Only the Clinic Owner records payments, advances and their use, and sees the finance figures.		UC-39, UC-45One accountable role handles money.Clinic Owner
GBR-063Payment
A payment cannot be dated in the future and cannot exceed what remains to be paid for each service.		UC-39No overpayment.Clinic Owner; system
GBR-064Lab fee first
Before other services on a sheet are paid, at least 30% of each lab fee must be paid; an advance is used for that lab part first.		UC-39, UC-35The clinic covers its lab cost first.System
GBR-065Using an advance
Money taken from an advance cannot exceed the advance balance.		UC-39Only money actually received is used.Clinic Owner
GBR-066Forfeit a no-show advance
The advance of a patient who did not come can be forfeited only when the sheet has no treatment work or lab order and the advance has not been used; a reason is required.		UC-39, UC-18Forfeit only money that was never used.Clinic Owner
GBR-067PayOS payment
A bank-transfer QR is valid for 30 minutes and only one can be active per sheet and purpose. The payment is recorded automatically when PayOS confirms it.		UC-39Online payments are recorded only when really received.System
GBR-068Unique catalog entries
Each catalog entry (service, diagnosis, medicine, customer group, material, supplier, lab company, lab work type) has a unique code and name.		UC-40, UC-41Entries are not confused with each other.Clinic Owner
GBR-069Deactivate instead of delete
An entry already used is deactivated, not deleted, and needs a reason; entries used by the system cannot be deactivated. Inactive entries cannot be chosen for new work. A material category that still has items cannot be deleted.		UC-40, UC-41Past records keep their catalog data.Clinic Owner
GBR-070Units
Quantities are converted to the base unit exactly: materials may have up to 3 decimals, medicines must be whole units.		UC-37, UC-38, UC-41, UC-42Stock and doses stay exact.System
GBR-071Purchases
A purchase cannot be entered for a future date; the received date is between the entry date and today; the expiry date is not before the entry date; the amount paid is not above the total. It uses an active material and an active supplier that is not a lab company.		UC-42Purchases reflect real deliveries.Clinic Owner, Clinic Assistant
GBR-072Stock balance
Stock on hand is the quantity received minus the quantity used and can never be negative.		UC-42, UC-37Stock shows real quantities.System
GBR-073Deactivate a branch
A branch cannot be deactivated while it has future appointments, waiting or ongoing visits, open treatment sheets or lab orders not delivered. The impact is shown first, a reason is required, and nothing is cancelled automatically.		UC-43Patients' open matters are never abandoned.Clinic Owner
GBR-074Dental chairs
A chair can be activated only in an active branch and cannot be deactivated while it still has obligations such as booked appointments.		UC-43Booked chairs stay available.Clinic Owner
GBR-075General settings history
Every settings change needs a reason and creates a new version; an older version can be restored as a new version. Changes apply to new records and the next printout, not to existing ones.		UC-44Settings changes are traceable and reversible.Clinic Owner
GBR-076Finance figures
Deleted sheets are not counted. A cancelled sheet keeps the money already collected and has no amount left to collect. Profit counts only fully paid sheets (revenue minus lab cost), minus the cost of material purchases.		UC-45Figures reflect money actually earned.System
GBR-077Work and service figures
The cost of each work line is quantity x price minus discount; paid and remaining amounts follow the payments of the sheet. Deleted sheets and deleted work are not counted.		UC-46Statistics match the treatment sheets.System
