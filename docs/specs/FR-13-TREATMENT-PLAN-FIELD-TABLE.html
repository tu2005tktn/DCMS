13.1 Treatment Plans (plan templates)

This screen (/treatment-templates, menu "Kế hoạch điều trị", group "Chuyên môn") allows a dentist to list the treatment plans of a branch, to create a plan, to open a plan and edit its items (service, teeth, time, cost), and to rename or delete it. A plan is a ready-made list of items that the dentist later applies to the treatment sheet of a patient. The permission "Xem mẫu điều trị" is needed to see the screen and "Tạo, sửa và phát hành mẫu điều trị" to create, edit and delete.
