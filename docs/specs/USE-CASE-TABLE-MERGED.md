# Bảng use case DCMS (bản gộp, 46 UC)

Mỗi dòng là một mục tiêu của một actor. Các dòng "Xem…" và CRUD danh mục đã được gộp vào use case cha.
Actor: Guest, Patient (tài khoản khách hàng), Clinic Assistant, Dentist, Clinic Owner, Admin, System (tác vụ nền).
Quy ước tên: "Manage X" chỉ dùng cho quản lý danh mục/đối tượng với các thao tác cùng loại (thêm, sửa, tìm, ẩn hoặc hiện). Quy trình nghiệp vụ dùng động từ cụ thể (Book, Reschedule, Plan, Record, Track...).
Nội dung dựa trên code hiện tại (backend `Features`, web, mobile). Các điểm đã chỉnh so với bản cũ: thu tiền chỉ Clinic Owner; tạo/sửa tài khoản nhân viên chỉ Admin; Dentist chỉ xem hồ sơ bệnh nhân; không có xóa tài liệu lâm sàng.

| ID | Use Case | Feature | Actor | Use Case Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UC-01 | Sign In / Sign Out | Account and Access | Patient, Admin, Dentist, Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner | Authenticate an account, establish a session with the appropriate permissions, renew the session, sign the staff member out automatically after the configured idle time, and end the session on sign-out (web and mobile). |
| UC-02 | Sign In with Google | Account and Access | Patient | Authenticate a patient with a Google account when Google sign-in is configured on the server. |
| UC-03 | Register Patient Account | Account and Access | Guest, Patient | Create a patient account with a name, a Vietnamese mobile number, an e-mail and a password that follows the password policy (web and mobile). |
| UC-04 | Recover Account | Account and Access | Patient, Dentist, Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner, Admin | Let a patient or a staff member who forgot the password verify the identity with a code and set a new password; an Admin can start the recovery for a staff account (web and mobile). |
| UC-05 | Manage Staff Accounts | Account and Access | Admin | Search, create and edit staff accounts (employee, role, branches), lock or unlock them, and review their change history. |
| UC-06 | Manage Access Groups | Account and Access | Clinic Owner, Admin | Create and edit access groups with their screen and action grants, preview the effect, activate or deactivate a group, and assign a staff member to a group and branches. |
| UC-07 | Receive Notifications | Notifications | Patient, Admin, Dentist, Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner | Receive notifications in real time and as push notifications in the mobile app (the web app has no notification screen), including appointment updates and the reminders the system sends 24 hours and 2 hours before a confirmed appointment; list them, see the unread count, mark them as read and open the related screen. |
| UC-08 | Book Appointment Online | Patient Booking | Patient | Book an examination for yourself or a relative: choose the examinee, the branch, the dentist (on web also "any dentist with a free chair") and a free time slot, confirm the contact, review and confirm (web in five steps, mobile in three steps). |
| UC-09 | Reschedule or Cancel My Appointment | Patient Booking | Patient | List and open upcoming and past appointments with their change history; reschedule or cancel at least 12 hours before the appointment, or send a support request when it is closer, and withdraw a pending request. |
| UC-10 | Link Account to Patient Record | Patient Record | Patient | Send a request to link the account to an existing patient record, follow its status and withdraw it (web and mobile). |
| UC-11 | View My Medical Record | Patient Record | Patient | See the basic information, visit results, treatment sheets and payments, images and documents, and prescriptions once the account is linked (web and mobile). |
| UC-12 | Update My Information | Patient Record | Patient | Update the administrative information of the linked patient record (mobile). |
| UC-13 | Manage Family Members | Patient Record | Patient | Add, edit, search and delete the family members saved in the account. |
| UC-14 | View Family Member Record | Patient Record | Patient | See the appointments booked for a family member and, when the clinic grants access, the record within the granted scopes (examinations, treatment, prescriptions, documents); request the access or withdraw the request. |
| UC-15 | View Appointment Calendar | Appointments | Clinic Assistant, Dentist, Clinic Owner | See the appointments of a branch in a day or week calendar, filter them by dentist and status, open the detail of an appointment and start the allowed actions from it. |
| UC-16 | Book Appointment for a Patient | Appointments | Clinic Assistant, Dentist | Create a standard or walk-in appointment for a patient, a new customer or a relative, with its source (phone, Zalo, Facebook, in person), choosing the dentist or any dentist with a free chair, with a reason when the lead-time rule is broken. |
| UC-17 | Reschedule Appointment | Appointments | Clinic Assistant, Dentist | Move an appointment to another time, dentist or chair from the calendar, the appointment detail or the patient profile. |
| UC-18 | Cancel Appointment or Mark No-show | Appointments | Clinic Assistant, Dentist | Cancel an appointment with a reason or record that the patient did not come, and book a walk-in replacement appointment in the freed time. |
| UC-19 | Book Next Visit | Appointments | Clinic Assistant, Dentist | Book the next visit of a patient from the profile or from a treatment sheet, using the next-visit candidates of the open treatment items, with the patient's consent. |
| UC-20 | Handle Support Request | Appointments | Clinic Assistant | Review the change or cancel requests of patients in the support inbox, verify the requester, apply the change, resolve it, or reject it with a reason. |
| UC-21 | Set and Publish Working Schedule | Scheduling | Clinic Owner, Dentist | Set a dentist's weekly template or a one-day exception with periods of work, break or block per branch; check the effect on confirmed appointments, preview the bookable slots, and publish. |
| UC-22 | Manage Appointment Settings | Scheduling | Clinic Owner | Set, for a branch, the slot interval and the services that can be booked online with their durations. |
| UC-23 | Check In Patient | Reception | Clinic Assistant | Find the appointment of the day, confirm the patient (or create the patient record at the desk), record the arrival time, purpose and note, and put the patient in the waiting queue; follow the queue (waiting, being examined, examination confirmed, completed). |
| UC-24 | Manage Patient Records | Patient Records | Clinic Assistant (Dentist, Clinic Owner: view only) | Search, create, edit, archive and restore patient records with the duplicate check and required fields, and open the profile (appointments, examinations, treatment and payments, documents, prescriptions). |
| UC-25 | Verify Record Link | Patient Records | Clinic Assistant | Check a request to link an account to a record, or a request for access to a family member's record, compare it with the patient record, and approve, reject or revoke it. |
| UC-26 | Handle Care Tasks | Customer Care | Clinic Assistant, Dentist | Create, edit and follow care tasks such as reminders and follow-up calls in a work list, with notes, contact attempts, escalation and export. |
| UC-27 | Conduct Examination | Examination | Dentist | Start a visit, record symptoms, history, the odontogram (2D/3D), findings and diagnoses, review earlier visits, save, confirm, amend, complete the visit and hand the patient over to treatment. |
| UC-28 | Order Investigations | Examination | Dentist, Clinic Assistant | Order an investigation (for example an X-ray) for a visit, receive the result, review it and cancel it. |
| UC-29 | Upload and Review Examination Documents | Examination | Dentist, Clinic Assistant | Upload, view and download the images and documents of a visit or a patient; the dentist reviews them. |
| UC-30 | Analyze X-ray with AI | Examination | Dentist | When the AI service is configured on the server, run it on an X-ray image to detect suspected caries, show the boxes and confidence as a suggestion, and keep the result with the document. |
| UC-31 | Plan Treatment | Treatment | Dentist, Clinic Owner | Create a treatment sheet (normal or quick), plan the items with planned dates (also from a plan template), copy an item, and revise the plan and the agreed prices. |
| UC-32 | Record Treatment Work | Treatment | Dentist, Clinic Owner | Record and confirm the work done for a plan item, add notes and images, and print the treatment sheet. |
| UC-33 | Close, Reopen or Cancel Treatment Sheet | Treatment | Dentist, Clinic Owner | Close a treatment sheet when the work is finished, reopen it, cancel it with its payment effect, or delete a sheet opened by mistake; see its history. |
| UC-34 | Manage Treatment Plan Templates | Treatment | Dentist, Clinic Owner | Create, rename, edit and delete the ready-made treatment plans of a branch and their items. |
| UC-35 | Order Lab Work | Treatment | Dentist, Clinic Owner | Create, edit or remove a lab order for a piece of work on a treatment sheet. |
| UC-36 | Track Lab Work | Treatment | Clinic Assistant, Clinic Owner | Follow the lab orders by status and move an order through sent, received, delivered or rework with a history; open the order. |
| UC-37 | Record Materials Used | Treatment | Dentist, Clinic Assistant | Record the materials used for a piece of work and take them out of the stock. |
| UC-38 | Prescribe Medicine | Treatment | Dentist | Create, view and edit a prescription for a patient with clinic stock medicines or medicines bought outside. |
| UC-39 | Collect Treatment Payment | Payment | Clinic Owner | Record a payment or an advance for a treatment sheet, pay online through a PayOS QR order, allocate an advance to the sheet, and record that the advance of a no-show patient is forfeited; payments made by PayOS are also confirmed automatically from the PayOS webhook and a reconcile job every 15 seconds. |
| UC-40 | Manage Clinic Catalogs | Administration | Clinic Owner, Dentist | Add, edit, search and deactivate or reactivate treatment services and prices, diagnoses, medicines and customer groups, with export where available. |
| UC-41 | Manage Supply Catalog | Administration | Clinic Owner | Add, edit, search and deactivate or delete material categories, instruments and materials, suppliers, lab companies and types of lab work. |
| UC-42 | Manage Dental Supplies | Administration | Clinic Owner, Clinic Assistant | Record purchases of instruments and materials with the supplier, batch, expiry date and payment; follow the stock and the usage history. |
| UC-43 | Manage Branches and Dental Chairs | Administration | Clinic Owner | Create and edit a branch, activate or deactivate it after checking the effect, and add, edit, activate or deactivate its chairs. |
| UC-44 | Manage General Settings | Administration | Clinic Owner | Set the staff idle sign-out time, the required patient fields, the patient code mode and prefix and the receipt print options, with preview, version history and restore; see who changed which setting. |
| UC-45 | View Finance Statistics | Reports | Clinic Owner | See the amount to collect, the money received, the advance, the receivables, the cost of materials and the profit, and open the list behind each figure. |
| UC-46 | View Work and Service Statistics | Reports | Clinic Owner | See the work and services of treatment sheets with the cost, the paid and the remaining amount, filtered by branch, period, status and search. |
