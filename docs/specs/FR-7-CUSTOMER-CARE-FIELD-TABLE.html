7.1 Customer Care

This screen (/customer-care, title "Chăm sóc khách hàng") is for clinic assistants and the clinic owner. It allows staff to find care tasks by period, owner, type, status or customer, to open a task and record a contact with the customer, to create or edit a task, and to export the list to Excel. The clinic owner can read every branch but does not write.
