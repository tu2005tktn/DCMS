1. Major Features

IDFeature NameDescription
MF-01Appointment Scheduling & Calendar ManagementCalendar-based scheduling for the clinic's dentists and treatment rooms across service types, with automatic conflict detection and daily/weekly schedule views.
MF-02Patient Self-Booking PortalA patient-facing web page where patients view real-time available time slots and book appointments directly; bookings are updated automatically into the clinic's central schedule.
MF-03Patient Records & Clinical Data ManagementCentralized storage of patient profiles, medical history, treatment history, dental charting, prescriptions, and clinical notes, retrievable instantly when a patient returns.
MF-04Integrated Dental Image ManagementStorage and viewing of X-ray and intraoral images (DICOM, JPEG, PNG) directly inside the patient's record, with side-by-side comparison of current and historical images without page reloads.
MF-05AI-Assisted Dental Image Analysis (Advance)AI analysis of uploaded dental images to detect and highlight potential abnormalities (caries, periodontal bone loss, periapical lesions) as annotated overlays with confidence scores; dentists can confirm, modify, or reject AI suggestions.
MF-06Billing and Payment ManagementInvoicing and payment recording supporting cash, bank transfer, and QR payments; revenue reports by dentist, service, and time period.
MF-07Inventory and Consumables ManagementTracking of dental materials and consumables with automatic low-stock alerts and usage history linked to treatments.
MF-08Medication and Follow-up RemindersA mobile application that allows patients to receive medication reminders, follow-up appointment reminders
